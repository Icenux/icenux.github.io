ccc Android.

Your Claude Code queue, on your phone.

The phone client of ccc TUI. Read your conversations, send messages, approve or deny tool calls, start new conversations and check the usage limits — from anywhere, privately over Tailscale. It is a native Kotlin / Jetpack Compose app and needs a running ccc on your computer.

What it does

Approve from
anywhere.

The app holds no secret: a one-time QR code gives the phone a token made for that phone alone. It is only a window into the ccc running on your computer.

Conversations

See every conversation with its state and queue, read the messages, send a new one or start a conversation in any project.

Approvals

When Claude waits for permission, approve or deny the tool call right from the phone — no need to get back to the terminal.

Task queue

The same queue as in the terminal: check what is waiting, what is running and what has finished.

Usage limits

Check how much of your Claude usage is left and when it resets, without opening the terminal.

Private by design

The phone talks to your computer over Tailscale. Nothing goes through a server of ours.

Download

Install ccc Android.

Needs Android 10 or newer and a running ccc TUI on your computer with its API turned on (--api). Open the APK on the phone — Android asks once to allow installs from that source.

Download .apk All releases ↗
Pair with your computer 
ccc api pair

Run it on the computer (or use Settings → Devices… → Pair a new device in ccc), then tap Scan QR code in the app.

Verify the download

Check the checksum and signatures 
sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS
gpg --import release-signing-key.asc && gpg --verify SHA256SUMS.asc SHA256SUMS
apksigner verify --print-certs ccc.apk

Download ccc.apk, SHA256SUMS and SHA256SUMS.asc from the release and release-signing-key.asc from the ccc_android repository. The GPG key's fingerprint must be {{ site.data.keys.ccc.fingerprint }}.
