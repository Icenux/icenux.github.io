Screensaver toggle

scrv.

One command to keep your screen awake. Run it once to disable the screensaver and lock, run it again to bring them back.

A simple bash script that stacks several inhibition layers — gsettings, xset, systemd-inhibit and a DBus polling fallback — so it works on X11 and Wayland, from Cinnamon and GNOME to Hyprland and Sway.

What it does

Many layers.
One toggle.

Each layer covers a different environment, and they all switch on and off together.

gsettings

Disables the screensaver and lock at the desktop-settings level on Cinnamon, GNOME and MATE.

xset (X11)

xset s off -dpms turns off the X screensaver and DPMS blanking. Silently skipped on Wayland.

systemd-inhibit

Idle inhibit at the logind level, independent of the desktop — natively respected by hypridle and swayidle on Wayland.

Polling fallback

Every 20 s it sends SimulateUserActivity over DBus and resets the X idle timer, for desktops the other layers miss.

Omarchy aware

When the omarchy CLI is present it calls omarchy toggle idle; on every other system that step is a no-op.

Clean state

The on/off state lives in a PID file. v1.5 also cleans up orphaned inhibit processes when you close the terminal it was toggled in.

Where it works

X11 and Wayland.

scrv applies the layers that matter for your session, so the same command works across desktops.

  • X11: Cinnamon, GNOME, MATE, KDE Plasma, XFCE, Openbox, i3, dwm
  • Wayland: Hyprland, Sway, GNOME, KDE Plasma
  • wlroots: labwc, river, niri (with swayidle)
  • Omarchy 4 “Quattro” via its own idle toggle
  • Non-systemd distros still work via xset, DBus and gsettings
  • Custom idle daemons such as xss-lock can’t be paused

Good to know

Known limits.

  • XWayland apps: xset only affects the XWayland context
  • The screensaver may flash briefly if it fires just before the script runs
  • Releasing the inhibit can take up to ~20 s
  • Hyprland with ignore_systemd_inhibit = true ignores the lock
  • Remote sessions (SSH, VNC without a desktop) may have no DBus session
  • Custom idle daemons started directly cannot be paused
